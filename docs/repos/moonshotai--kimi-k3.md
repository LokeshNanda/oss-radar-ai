## What it does
Kimi K3 is a multimodal AI model designed for tasks involving long-horizon coding, knowledge work, and reasoning. It features a 2.8 trillion parameter architecture and supports a context window of up to one million tokens, allowing it to process and understand text, images, and video simultaneously.

## Why it's interesting
Kimi K3 claims to be the world's first open 3T-class model, emphasizing its open-weight nature and advanced architecture, which includes Kimi Delta Attention and a mixture-of-experts approach. Its ability to handle complex tasks with minimal human oversight sets it apart from other AI models.

## How it works
The architecture is based on a mixture-of-experts (MoE) framework, activating 16 out of 896 experts for improved efficiency. It includes 93 layers, with a combination of KDA and gated MLA attention mechanisms.
