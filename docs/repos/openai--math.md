## What it does
This repository contains mathematical manuscripts and proof artifacts produced by an internal OpenAI model, focusing on various mathematical problems. It includes results at different stages of verification, with some outputs building upon earlier model results.

## Why it's interesting
Unlike typical mathematical repositories, this one leverages an internal AI model to generate and evaluate mathematical proofs, providing a unique intersection of AI and mathematics. The collection is organized into families of related papers, which is not a common structure in traditional mathematical literature.

## How it works
The results were generated using an unreleased internal OpenAI model, with each result requiring an average of three hours of compute time. The repository includes a Lean library for formal proofs, with approximately 42% of the results formalized. The manuscripts are organized into families based on related topics, and the repository is continuously updated with new formalizations as they become available. However, not all results are formally verified, and some may contain issues.
