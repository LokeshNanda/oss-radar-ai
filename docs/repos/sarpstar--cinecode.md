## What it does
Cinecode is a tool that allows users to create films using code, where an AI agent writes the script, generates visuals, and designs sound, ultimately producing an MP4 file. It supports various formats including short films, YouTube videos, ads, explainers, and music videos.

## Why it's interesting
Cinecode differentiates itself by combining AI-driven scriptwriting and visual generation with user-defined parameters, allowing for unique film outputs that are not merely replications of existing works. It also offers a variety of modes tailored for different types of video content, from quick social media clips to more complex cinematic productions.

## How it works
Users provide a brief idea in a single sentence, after which the AI agent asks questions to refine the concept, including format, style, and sound. It then generates a plan, renders a draft, and allows for user feedback before finalizing the film. The tool operates in two modes: a quick mode for shorter projects and a film mode for longer, more detailed productions.
