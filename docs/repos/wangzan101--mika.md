## What it does
Mika is an on-device AI agent for Android that automates tasks across apps and the web based on user commands. It operates in a hidden background screen, allowing users to continue using their phone while it performs actions like sending messages, summarizing chats, or finding products online.

## Why it's interesting
Mika differentiates itself by not requiring app APIs or specific integrations; it mimics human interaction with the device's UI. It also emphasizes privacy by processing data locally without a central server, which is a notable feature compared to many cloud-based AI solutions.

## How it works
Mika utilizes a background screen to interact with apps and websites, reading and simulating user actions. It connects to various AI model providers for processing commands and can operate on Android 11 or later, with additional features available on Android 12 and above.
