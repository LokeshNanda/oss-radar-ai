## What it does
This repository contains a free online book titled "FDE: The Guidance Book of Forward Deployed Engineer," which serves as a comprehensive guide for understanding the role of Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) in the context of artificial intelligence. It discusses the definition, responsibilities, and real-world applications of FDEs, supported by case studies and data.

## Why it's interesting
The book is unique in that it specifically addresses the emerging role of FDEs in the AI landscape, providing insights based on extensive research and real-world examples. It aims to fill a knowledge gap in a rapidly evolving field, particularly in regions where this role is still gaining recognition.

## How it works
The book is structured into chapters that cover various aspects of the FDE role, including problem-solving, client engagement, deployment, and revenue expansion. Each chapter is linked to a Markdown file in the repository, allowing for easy navigation and reading. The author emphasizes the importance of verifiable data and case studies, which are indexed in an appendix.
